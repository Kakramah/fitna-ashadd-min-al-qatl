# الصور المستخدمة

ولّدت أداة الصور ثلاث صور أصلية للمشروع وفُحصت بصرياً. هذه النسخ تصوّر فني، ولا تُعرض بوصفها وثائق لأحداث جارية.

## hero-thread.webp
Photoreal editorial still. A single frayed dark-red cotton thread stretches across torn cream paper and touches a tiny ember. Part of a real adult hand reaches from the bottom edge. Leica SL2-S, 50mm, f/2.8, Kodak Portra 400. Upper-left light 3600K, paper fiber and cotton fuzz visible, natural skin pores, deep charcoal and warm ivory with muted ember red. Right-side dark negative space for HTML Arabic title. No text, logos, flags, signatures, avatars or fantasy glow.

## rumor-phone.webp
Documentary-style interior of an ordinary Syrian home. A face-down phone on a worn table, red cotton thread leading toward two distant adults passing separately in a corridor. Leica SL2-S, 50mm, f/2.8, Kodak Portra 400. Left window light 4300K, imperfect plaster and wood, restrained texture. No readable text or identifiable event.

## mended-thread.webp
Two adult hands carefully knot a frayed red cotton thread on cream paper. Leica SL2-S, 50mm, f/2.8, Kodak Portra 400. Upper-left morning light 4600K, natural skin, paper fibers, physically plausible knot. Dark negative space to the right for HTML text. No text, logos, flags, signatures or avatars.
